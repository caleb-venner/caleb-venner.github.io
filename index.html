About

I'm a final-year Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Software student at the University of Technology Sydney, finishing in mid-2027 with a High Distinction average and Dean's List recognition. I'm most interested in embedded systems and firmware: working out how a piece of hardware actually works, then writing the code that runs closest to it.

My recent work includes reverse-engineering an aquarium light's LED driver board and replacing its Bluetooth controller with an ESP32-S3 running my own ESP-IDF firmware in C. Before that, I built a Home Assistant integration that talks to the same light's stock firmware over Bluetooth LE.

I'm looking for a junior embedded or firmware engineering role with a smaller, focused team from mid-2027, and I'm open to casual or part-time work in the meantime.
