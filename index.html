About

I'm in the final year of a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Software Engineering at the University of Technology Sydney, with a High Distinction average and Dean's List recognition in 2024 and 2025. I'm most interested in embedded systems and firmware: working out how a piece of hardware actually works, then writing the code that runs closest to it.

My recent work includes reverse-engineering an aquarium light's LED driver board and replacing its Bluetooth controller with an ESP32-S3 running my own ESP-IDF firmware in C. Before that, I built a Home Assistant integration that talks to the same light's stock firmware over Bluetooth LE. At home I also run a Linux homelab on Proxmox and Raspberry Pi, deployed from Git.

I'm looking for a junior embedded, firmware or systems engineering role, and I'm available to start full-time now. My degree formally completes in mid-2027, but the one remaining subject has no set class times.
